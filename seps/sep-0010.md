# SEP-10: Contribution scoring (spec only, no payouts)

- Status: Draft
- Criteria: 0/4 done

## Motivation

Providers, strategy authors and indicator authors compete for the same future rewards, so one published rule must score them all from the same verifiable evidence. v1 scored by holder votes and team judgment, and the 2025 relaunch thread never settled who earns emissions. This produces a report only; SEP-1.10 still holds and nothing is paid until a legal structure exists.

## Criteria

- [ ] SEP-10.1: One published scoring rule computes, from signed and anchored history only, a score per contributor (provider, strategy author, indicator author) per period, and two independent runs produce byte-identical reports.
  Probe: test: scoring on a fixture history, two machines. Pass: byte-identical report. Tool: pytest + diff.
- [ ] SEP-10.2: Score flows down the dependency chain: a signal's forward performance is split between its provider, the strategy it came from and the indicators that strategy pins by hash; a fixture with known usage yields the expected split.
  Probe: test: fixture: 2 indicators, 1 strategy, 1 provider. Pass: expected split. Tool: pytest.
- [ ] SEP-10.3: Anti: a backtest result, or performance before a contribution's anchored publication time, adds to any score.
  Probe: property: backtest or pre-anchor results injected. Pass: score unchanged. Tool: hypothesis.
- [ ] SEP-10.4: Anti: splitting one contribution or one track record across several identities raises the combined score (property test: split-neutral).
  Probe: property: random splits across identities. Pass: combined score not higher. Tool: hypothesis.
