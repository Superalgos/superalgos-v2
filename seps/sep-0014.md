# SEP-14: Follower risk controls

- Status: Draft
- Criteria: 0/4 done

## Motivation

v1 had balance caps but no risk concept in 2,099 doc pages; nobody should follow a stranger with real money without limits. Required before live trading (SEP-12.5).

## Criteria

- [ ] SEP-14.1: A follower sets, per followed provider, a spend cap (percent of balance) and a minimum and maximum balance; an order that would breach them is not placed and the ledger says which limit stopped it.
  Probe: test: orders breaching spend cap / min / max balance. Pass: not placed; ledger names the limit. Tool: pytest.
- [ ] SEP-14.2: A follower sets a daily loss limit (absolute or percent); once reached, the bot stops opening positions until the next UTC day or a manual resume, and says so in its status.
  Probe: test: losses reaching the daily limit; next UTC day. Pass: stops opening; resumes next day or manually. Tool: pytest (fake clock).
- [ ] SEP-14.3: An execution error (exchange reject, reconciliation mismatch, missing data) pauses the affected provider's follow after a configurable count, instead of retrying blindly; resume is explicit.
  Probe: test: N injected execution errors. Pass: provider follow paused; explicit resume. Tool: pytest.
- [ ] SEP-14.4: A kill switch (CLI, dashboard, and later the mobile app) cancels open orders and stops all following in one action, and survives a restart (the bot comes back stopped).
  Probe: e2e: kill switch, then restart. Pass: orders cancelled; bot comes back stopped. Tool: pytest + process restart.
